About

I'm a computer science student at Stanford interested in empirical security and AI security. A recurring question in my work is whether the signals we trust — model confidence, security guidance, compliance checks, or system safeguards — actually correspond to security under the conditions where they matter.

I especially enjoy projects where the obvious metric says everything is working and the interesting part begins when you ask what that metric is hiding.
