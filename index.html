Research

ContainmentBench

In progress

I'm building an evaluation environment for testing whether security controls can contain autonomous coding agents across benign and adversarial multi-step tasks. I'm especially interested in where pointwise controls break down once behavior unfolds over a longer trajectory.

Hidden Reliability Failures in Malware Detection

I evaluated the released EMBER2024 malware detector across 1.08 million Windows PE files. Overall, it looked excellent: 98.0% accuracy and an ECE of 0.0031. Looking by malware family told a different story, including families with false-negative rates above 80%.

Predicting Vulnerability Attributes from CVE Descriptions

I'm studying how much structured vulnerability information can be recovered from the CVE description alone. On held-out 2024 CVEs, simple TF-IDF baselines beat zero-shot 70B+ language models on severity and CWE prediction, while rare labels and attributes that required more implicit inference remained much harder.

Compliance Reality Checker

Security controls can look enabled without actually being enforced. I built checks for Linux and Microsoft 365 that compare global policy with account-level exceptions and runtime state.

Publications

Investigating Usefulness of Security Threat Reports

Manuscript under review

We studied incident reports from DFIR firms and cyberinsurers to ask how much defenders can actually learn by comparing them. Even basic estimates of common initial-access vectors and exploited vulnerabilities often disagreed enough to make simple conclusions surprisingly difficult to justify.

A First Look at Governments’ Enterprise Security Guidance

USENIX Security '25 · August 2025 · Research contributor

A systematic study of government enterprise-security guidance across 41 countries. Among ten frameworks examined in depth, only 2 of 166 observed security controls were universally recommended.

I contributed to the research and writing and am acknowledged in the paper; I am not a listed author.
